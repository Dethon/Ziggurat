# Chat hub: people, topics, streams and spaces

See also: `.claude/rules/channels.md`, `.claude/rules/chat-queue.md`, `.claude/rules/telegram.md`, `.claude/rules/channel-attachments.md`.

- **The chat hub's person is the proxy's, settled as the connection arrives.** Caddy's `forward_auth` (the accounts service's session check) copies its four headers (`ForwardedAccount`, `Domain/Security`) over anything the client sent, before every request to the hub and its upload and dictation endpoints. `SignedInConnectionFilter` verifies only the signed `X-Ziggurat-Assertion` (channel `signalr`, `.claude/rules/person-assertions.md`), never the plain three; a connection without one is closed "not signed in", and `InvokeMethodAsync` refuses any call on a connection holding none, so a new hub method is covered by default. There is no registration call. `SenderId` and the push subscriptions' key are the account's opaque user id. A host with no proxy in front (`Local: MCP Channel SignalR`) refuses every connection.
  - **A turn needs the accounts service's word of its own**, since a connection outlives its ten-minute assertion by hours: every send path (`SendMessage`, `SendRerun`, `SendContinue`, `EnqueueMessage`) takes a `SenderSnapshot` as it accepts the message and vouches, writes and emits from it alone (so a dispatched queued message carries what a live send does), and through `PersonTurns` asks a fresh assertion for the connection's **session** (`ForwardedAccount.SessionId`, `ISessionAccounts`, `/accounts/internal/session`) — never for the account by id — as the turn is sent (a queued one also as queued), carried as the notification's `Assertion`. A revoked session (or none named) ends the turn `TopicRefusals.SignedOut`, an unreachable service "could not be checked right now"; nothing is sent (at queueing the refusal is its sender's alone, `.claude/rules/chat-queue.md`). A turn already running finishes.
  - **And its connection is closed at once**: every way sessions end is announced as `SessionsEnded { tenantId, userId }` on `SessionsEnded.EndedChannel` (`accounts:sessions:ended`); `EndedSessionsListener`/`SessionConnections` abort the held sessions the service no longer vouches for (unreachable closes nothing).
- **A chat-client turn acts for the account and goes by its name.** The hub stamps `ChannelMessageNotification.Principal` itself (`ForwardedAccount.PrincipalOn("signalr")`). `Sender` is the display name asked once on arrival (`IAccountResolver`, `.claude/rules/channel-identity.md`); one it could not get leaves the account id and never refuses the connection. `Sender` is a label, never a key: whatever must find the person reads the `Account` — `IAgentFactory.Create`, memory (`.claude/rules/memory-architecture.md`), and a history's authorship (`ChatMessage.GetAuthorId()`: `Principal.Account` on a turn with no `OriginKind`, else the sender), because the client tells its own messages by account id.
- **A topic is its owner's unless they share it, and an owner may grant someone every topic of the household.** `TopicMetadata` records `OwnerId`, `TenantId`, `Shared` and `OwnerName` (a label, never a key). `TopicViewer` (built by `ForwardedAccount.Viewer`) is the one rule, applied by hub, store and chat client alike:
  - A viewer **sees** their own topics, every unowned topic recording their household (a voice guest's records `CreateConversationParams.GuestTenantId`, set off the verified identity; pre-owner topics the household migration shared and gave an owner, `.claude/rules/household-migration.md`), their household's shared topics, and with `sees-all` every topic of their household; one recording no household is nobody's. They **may continue** (send, stop, upload) own, shared and unowned topics they see — seeing all is reading, never deciding — and **may rename or delete** only their own, or an unowned one they see (`TopicViewer.MayContinue`, `MayRenameOrDelete`). Marking read is open to any viewer.
  - `GetTopicPageAsync`/`SearchTopicsAsync` take the viewer and fill a page from below whatever they passed over (`RedisThreadStateStore.FillPageAsync`), so another's private topics never cut a page short.
  - **A list may be narrowed by whose topics it shows** (`TopicFilter`; `TopicViewer.Lists` = `Sees` and the filter, so a filter only narrows and needs no permission) via `GetTopicPageFiltered`/`SearchTopicsFiltered`; `GetTopicPage`/`SearchTopics` stay for installed apps from before (a hub binds every argument). A sees-all person reads `Mine` by default, picks another from `GetHouseholdPeople` (empty for anyone else), remembered per device (`TopicFilterEffect`). The client's store holds every row to the same rule (`TopicsState.Lists`): a filtered-out row loses its badge and row but stays open if open — only one no longer seen is closed. A conversation started while the list shows another person's moves the list to Mine once saved (`TopicListMoves`; user's decision, 2026-11-15).
  - **Every topic key names its household** (`RedisThreadStateStore`): `topic@{tenantId}:…`, `topics@{tenantId}:{agentId}:{slug}`, `topicdoc@{tenantId}:…` (field `household` in `idx:household-topics`, filtered by the searcher's), and the hub passes the connection's `ForwardedAccount.TenantId` to `GetTopicAsync(tenantId, …)`, never a client's word. The '@' keeps these apart from the pre-household shapes the household migration's patterns match. The one household-free key is `topic-of:{agentId}:{chatId}:{threadId}` (valued `{tenantId}:{topicId}`), so `GetTopicByChatIdAndThreadIdAsync`'s caller applies `TopicViewer`, and the hub's save and delete check it first: another household's topic over the conversation fails the rule rather than being overwritten or deleted. Histories stay keyed by conversation. No household is `RedisThreadStateStore.NoHousehold`.
  - Every hub call naming a topic, session or approval resolves the stored topic first and refuses one the caller may not see with "No such conversation." — the words for one that does not exist (`StartSession` answers false). A conversation **no topic holds** is read by nobody (`GetHistory` empty, since Telegram's numeric ids are guessable) and opened, saved or deleted only when its own topic id names it (`ConversationIdGenerator.CreateFor`). A new topic saved from the client is the connection's person's and private, whatever the browser sent; `SetTopicShared` is the owner's alone.
  - **Approvals:** shown to whoever sees the topic, settled by the asker's account alone (`ApprovalService.AskerOf`, off the hop's `_meta`) from any of their connections; one acting for nobody by the topic's owner, on a shared topic also whoever's turn is running, on an unowned one anyone who sees it. Anyone else is answered false and it stays pending. Only the asker gets buttons (`ToolApprovalRequestMessage.AskedBy`, `ApprovalCard`).
  - **Reach:** every connection joins `space:{tenant}:{slug}` and exactly one of `…:user:{id}` or, holding `sees-all`, `…:sees-all` (`TopicAudience`); a topic recording no household reaches no group. A private topic's news goes to its owner's group and the sees-all group, any other's to the space, a share change to the whole space so each client adds or drops the row by `TopicViewer`. A person's message announces its stream there (`OnStreamStarted`, as `create_conversation` does), carrying `UserMessageInfo.SenderName` (shown for anybody but the reader and the topic's starter); the sending browser ignores it.
  - **Push:** a private topic's push goes to its owner's subscriptions alone (`SendToPersonAsync`), any other's to its household's space. It carries the reply's last text as one line (`StreamService.PushExcerptLength`), never reasoning or tool calls, and names `PushNotice.Topic` beside the space's address, which the worker (`push-handler.js`) compares with the page on screen to stay quiet; a tap opens `/{space}?agent=&topic=` (`LaunchLink`). Keys: `push:space@{tenant}:{slug}` (`RedisPushSubscriptionStore`), a person's under their user id; only its owner moves or removes one. Each carries the subscribing session (`PushSubscriptionDto.SessionId`, stamped by the hub, never the browser's word), asked about before each push (`WebPushNotificationService`): a phone whose session ended is dropped, one it could not ask about skipped. A question asked in a topic (`RequestApprovalAsync`) is pushed to its asker's devices whether or not a page is open; an offered approval and an alert push to `/` with no conversation, the offer only when the person has no chat client open.
  - **A failed turn is kept**: the message and `TurnFailure.Answer`, both `IsFailedTurn` (`RedisChatMessageStore.InvokedCoreAsync`), shown as an error with retry (`ChatHistoryMessage.IsError`), never read by the model (`ModelView`) or memory's extraction, replaced by a retry sending the same words (`DropTailAsync`, which takes the count back too).
  - **A turn a service started is a line, not a message** (`ServiceNotice`): a service's sender (`ServiceSenders`) or a request with a response format (`ChatHistoryMessage.ResponseFormat` — how the Sugary app's turns, which carry the person's account, are told apart). Its prompt toggle shows a
    fire's instruction alone (`FireReports.InstructionOf`), never the report framed beside it.
  - A topic an agent opens (`create_conversation`) buffers its prompt with its time and announces `OnStreamStarted` after `OnTopicChanged`. Opening a topic always asks the store to mark it read, since the client's count can be older than the store's.
  - **How far a topic was read is each person's** (`TopicMetadata.ReadBy`, written inside the mark-read script; `ReadFor(userId)` applied by store and client; the old shared `ReadPosition` is the fallback for whoever has none): one person's devices share a position, told to the topic's audience as a rename is, and a member reading a shared topic clears nothing for anyone else (user's decision, 2026-11-05).
  - **A turn ended by a rejected approval** has no text, so its tool result (`ApprovalRejection`) draws as an assistant line. One JSON object draws as `JsonOutline`; a named format may add its own view (`StructuredAnswers`).
  - **Review copies** are owned by the turn: the agent hands `create_conversation` the turn's `Principal` and `ConversationFactory` makes the topic the account's and private, or the voice guest's household's and shared, or (acting for nobody) no household's. The asking channel never names where the copy goes nor whose it is (ADR 0046). **A group's copy is shared**: the channel states a fact, `ReviewCopy.Group`, never a destination; it reaches `create_conversation` as `group` (voice's tool declares none; the MCP server ignores an undeclared argument), and the topic is nobody's and shared, the group's bound household recorded, while the turn still acts for its sender. Voice's topics are minted by the agent from the speaker's assertion (`.claude/rules/voice.md`).
- **A conversation is rewritten from its page, and keeps what a rewrite replaced** (`.scratch/chat-edits/spec.md`). Every stored message carries an entry id (`ChatMessage.GetEntryId`, stamped by `RedisChatMessageStore`; a retry keeps the replaced one's), projected by `GetHistory`; one from before ids is named `legacy:{index}:{hash}` (`HistoryEntries`), never stored. The agent's list stays the one line model and pages read; what a rewrite puts aside is a **version** of its slot under `agent-versions:{agentId}:{chatId}:{threadId}`. Rewrites are Domain's (`HistoryRewrite`); a failed turn is never a version. `RedisThreadStateStore.RewriteAsync` applies one in a transaction, refused when the line moved past the page's last entry id or a turn is in flight (`agent-turn:…`, set as a turn reads its history, cleared when kept and as its stream ends). The hub's rewrite calls (`DeleteFrom`, `PutAsideFrom`, `PutAsideReply`, `EditInPlace`, `SwitchVersion`) are for whoever may continue, refused while it streams or on a topic another channel still shows (`TopicMirrors`, `TopicMetadata.Mirrored`, set for review copies and voice), delete files neither the line nor a version still holds (`Rewritten.AttachmentsNoLongerHeld`), and tell the audience `OnHistoryChanged`, on which every page rereads. A reroll is `SendRerun` (`ChannelMessageNotification.RerunOf`): the person's message rides as itself, read once and kept once; a reply keeps its model (`ChatMessage.GetModel`). `Fork` is for whoever sees the topic (`HistoryFork`): a new private topic of the forker's, files copied into its own conversation.
- **A stopped reply is kept** as far as it got, on every channel — tool calls with results, text marked `ChatMessage.IsInterrupted` (also `ChatFinishReason.Length`), which the model reads — only while the line still ends where the turn read it, so a delete or clear that stopped it is never undone. **Continue** (`SendContinue`, `ChannelMessageNotification.Continuation`) carries on an interrupted last reply with a fixed ask (`Continuation.Instruction`) marked `IsContinuation`, which the model reads and nothing shows, counts or remembers (`Transcript.IsShown`, `ExtractionWindow`). `Transcript.StartsPersonsTurn` is the one turn-boundary rule, so a continued reply is one reply to every rewrite and fork (`ContinuesPrevious`, drawn by the client in the bubble it continues), and Retry after a failed Continue continues again. The page learns a streamed turn's entry ids from the history read after its stream ends (`EntryIdsLearned`, asked again a few times, since a turn — a stopped one above all — can be kept after its stream ended), never reordering what it shows, and skips the echo of its own rewrite.
- **One stream, several pages.** A page joining is handed what the stream carried under the same lock as its subscription (`StreamService.Join`): a joining send everything, a resume what came since the state the page drew (`ResumeStreamFrom` with `StreamSeen`; `ResumeStream` stays for older pages). A person's message carries the sending page's `UserMessageInfo.CorrelationId`: that page skips it (unless it queued it, `.claude/rules/chat-queue.md`); others draw it unless shown (`BufferRebuildUtility.IsShown`), and one shown closes nothing. A rename is told to the topic's audience as a share change is.
  - **A turn over within milliseconds can end before the page has a wire on it** (the fake model shows it). The losing send draws what the holder never finalized (`StreamingService.ShowWhatTheHolderMissesAsync`); a turn that came on no wire is inserted from the history (`EntryIdNaming.WithMissedTurns`), moving nothing, and only for a read at the messages' `Revision`, so a read from before a delete never puts it back.
  - A reply streams to a page only while its person may see the topic (`StreamService.AudienceChanged`, each wire re-asking `TopicViewer.Sees`); a page that may no longer see it only stops reading (`TopicDeleteEffect`), never asks the hub to stop a reply not its own.
- **A topic the hub says does not exist for this person is closed on their page.** After a reconnect (including the renewal `OnAccessChanged` asks for) the open topic's history is asked again, and `TopicRefusals.NoSuchConversation` (shared by hub and client) closes it as a delete does (`ReconnectionEffect`).
- **Spaces are the household's configuration** — its `spaces` section (`.claude/rules/household-config.md`), a new household's being the one `default` space; the client's host keeps no list and reads no header. **The hub reads its chat spaces alone** (`SpaceListing.ChatSpaces`, in `ChatHub.HouseholdSpacesAsync`): an app space (`HouseholdSpace.AppId`, `.claude/rules/apps.md`) holds no topics, is never in `GetSpaces`' answer (installed clients from before know no app spaces) and is "No such space." to `JoinSpace`; a topic kept under a slug an app space took is a removed space's, listed in the default space. **The space switcher asks `GetSpacesAndApps`** (`SwitcherEntry`, `SpaceSwitcher.Entries`): the household's spaces in its order, an app space among them with its app's link, then the person's own published apps no space shares, marked `Private`; the apps are the apps server's (`IPersonsApps`, taken by the method as `[FromServices]`; `PersonsAppsClient` asks the session refresh on `persons-apps` for the connection's session and the apps server at `APPSURL` with the app ids the spaces name, within `AppsTimeout`). Not asked (no `APPSURL`, the session gone, an outage, a refusal, slow) costs the apps alone: an app space with no link is left out, the chat spaces answered, never a `HubException` for it. `GetSpaces` stays the chat spaces alone, byte for byte, and the client still validates and joins on it. The client keeps the list in `SpaceState.Switcher`, asked **after the first load's own calls** (`InitializationEffect` dispatches `LoadSwitcher` last, however that load ends: the hub answers a connection's invocations one at a time, and this one waits on the apps server), on becoming live again and as the switcher opens (`SpaceEffect`), never on a space switch; not live it keeps the last list silently. `SpaceSwitcher` (the header's mark, a plain mark for one place) links a chat space to its address and an app to its origin, a top-level navigation; the app glyph is `Icons.Apps`. `GetSpaces` and `JoinSpace` (an unknown slug: "No such space.") ask the accounts `spaces` view with the session refresh's assertion (`ISessionAccounts.SessionAsync`), never the browser's word nor the arrival assertion. The manifest (`/manifest.webmanifest?slug=&name=&color=`, served by `WebChat/Program.cs`) is named by the page once it has heard from the hub (`manifestHelper.setSpace`), each value held to a space's shapes. A space opens (`SpaceAgent`, client side; the hub supplies `defaultAgent` and `IsHouseholdDefault`) on the agent last picked in it (localStorage `selectedAgentId:{slug}`; the default space keeps the old shared `selectedAgentId`), else the space's `defaultAgent` when the catalog holds it, else — in any other space — the old shared pick, else the household's default (its agents section's `defaultAgent`, else its first: `AgentCatalogEntry.IsHouseholdDefault`), else the first agent; a default the client chose is never stored as a pick (`SelectAgent.Picked`). **A removed space's topics are listed in the default space, read side** (`SpaceListing`): nothing is moved. `JoinSpace` on the default space asks once per join (never per page) which slugs hold topics (`SpacesHoldingTopicsAsync`) and passes the orphans as `alsoSpaces`, paged as one list with one cursor; their news is told to the default space's groups (`HeardIn`). A space added back under its slug has its topics again.
