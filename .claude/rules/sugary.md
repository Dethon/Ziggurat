# Sugary, per person

Sugary's log is SQLite in its own volume (`docs/adr/0043`); its channel is in `.claude/rules/channels.md` (dual-role servers), its mount in `.claude/rules/virtual-filesystem.md`. This file is who the log belongs to (`.scratch/accounts/spec.md`, "Sugary").

## Who owns a row

- Meals, doses, pens, periods, day notes, exercise sessions, push subscriptions, the profile, body measurements, the glucose link, glucose readings and sensors are `IPersonal` (`UserId` + `TenantId`, the accounts service's opaque ids); foods and recipes are `IHousehold`. Readings and sensors have no key into the link, so unlinking keeps them; a meal item's food/recipe id and a dose's meal ids are plain values, not keys. A glucose watch (`GlucoseWatch`) and its trigger states (`GlucoseWatchState`) are `IHousehold`; who is shown a watch — its subject, its writer, a holder of everyone's log — is said in `SqliteGlucoseWatches.ListAsync`, not by a filter (`SugaryWatchFileSystem`, `sugary-watches` at `/watchers`).
- `SugaryDbContext` holds every query to one owner with **query filters** on its `Owner`, so no route, mount read, find, update or delete carries a predicate of its own. Open it for a person with `SugaryStore.For(account)`. The factory's context (`Owner` null) owns nothing: it reads no row through the filters, and `SaveChanges` throws on a row it adds that names no owner.
- **Only these say `IgnoreQueryFilters()`**: the pen reminders, the import's already-there check, a push subscription changing hands, `GlucosePoller`, `GlucoseImport` and `GlucoseWatchEngine` (each spelling the owner out in every query), and `GlucoseWatchDelegationStore` (every household's watches, rewriting the delegation column alone).

## Rows from before

Rows from before the log was per person, and every row the `import` verb brings, carry **`legacy`** (`Owners.Unassigned`) in each owner column — spelled out in the `People` migration's defaults, which must never follow the constant. No account id can be `legacy`, so those rows are **nobody's**: no request, turn or reminder sees them. The household migration gives them to the person named `--sugary` (`HouseholdMigration/Steps/SugaryLog.cs`, `.claude/rules/household-migration.md`); `SugaryServer.GiveUnassignedRowsToAsync` is the suite's stand-in for that step. A legacy push subscription is taken over by whoever next signs in on that phone (the app saves its subscription on every open).

## The app and its API

The app has no sign-in. `sugary.*` in the Caddyfile puts the accounts service's session check in front of everything but `/mcp` (404) and `/accounts/*` (routed, so sign-in is same-origin; `/accounts/internal/*` is 404 first), and the check overwrites its four `X-Ziggurat-*` headers on the request. The server believes only `X-Ziggurat-Assertion`, verified against `Accounts:JwksUrl` for channel `sugary` (`Person.SignedInAsync`, `.claude/rules/person-assertions.md`); **the plain three are never read**. An `/api` call without a verifying assertion naming a person (the guest is nobody here) is **401** before any route binds — also the app's cue to sign in. Calling `localhost:6017` directly (ports override, E2E stack) needs an assertion: ask `http://localhost:5004/accounts/verify?channel=sugary` with a `mint-session` cookie and send the headers it answers.

- A route's `SugaryDbContext` parameter is **bound**, never resolved from the container (`SugaryDbContext.BindAsync`, `App/PersonsLog.cs`), so a route cannot be handed the unowned context. A route that needs the person itself takes `Person` too.
- **A person's log changes run one at a time** (`OneLogWriteAtATime`: meals, doses, pens, exercise, periods, day notes, glucose; never an estimate or a label, which wait on a model). A route that waits on somebody else before writing (LibreLinkUp linking) is marked `TakesItsOwnTurn` and holds her turn around its write alone (`TurnAsync`). The pantry (`/foods`, `/recipes`) queues per household. A refused save is re-read on the page (`refusedBecause`) and worded in her language.
- **A sheet's save keeps what somebody else saved meanwhile** (user's decision, 2026-11-05, merging per field; doses, pens, exercise, periods and the profile, saved field by field, too). App and server share this contract: the app sends the whole record plus what the sheet opened with (`loaded`, `stale.ts`), and each field she left as shown goes back as recorded (`overRecord`) — times shown to the minute, notes trimmed, a glucose range shown rounded in mmol/L — or an untouched field reads as her change. The server lays only the changed fields over the record as it is now (`StaleEdits`, `Domain/Editing/EditMerge`; a recipe's ingredients by food) and compares a note, name and a dose's meals as it would keep them; a meal's photo and dose are not compared. A field both changed differently is **409**, nothing saved (`errorChangedElsewhere`); a save with nothing loaded is saved as sent.

## Glucose

- **Her link** (`GlucoseLink`, `/api/glucose`): `PUT /link` logs in and lists followees before anything is kept, so a refusal leaves the old link; the region kept is the one LibreLinkUp found the account at. The password is sealed (`GlucoseCredentials`, AES-GCM under `SealingKey` ← compose `SEALINGKEY` ← `.env` `SUGARY_SEALING_KEY`, associated data her household's and her own id) and **no route answers it**. Without the key linking is 503 `linking-off` and the rest of Sugary works. Every refusal carries a `code` the app words (`GlucoseEndpoints` lists them).
- **The poller** (`GlucosePoller`, `GlucosePollLoop`, `Glucose:PollSeconds`) writes only where `LinkedAt` and `PatientId` are still what the poll read. **A password this server cannot open** is recorded as the link failing, `CredentialsUnreadable` (the app's `credentials-unreadable` asks her to link again), never skipped silently; it stays live on the doubling backoff, so a key put back opens it at its next ask with nothing to link again. **A household whose features turn Sugary off is polled as any** (user's decision, 2026-11-18): the feature takes Sugary from its agents and estimates, never the app; only its watches go quiet. `FailureDetail` is shown by the app, so it is a component or a minimum version, never a reason. After each poll that wrote, `IGlucosePollObserver` (`GlucoseWatchEngine`) is told.
- **Watches are judged by the server** (`GlucoseWatchEngine`, rules in `GlucoseTriggers`, Domain, pure; clock pass `GlucoseWatchLoop`), on her own readings only (`current`, `graph`), never `import`/`import-hourly`. **A Sugary-off household, or a person Sugary is off for, has nothing judged or fired** and its state rows are deleted (`SugaryOff`, the `sugary-off` view, `.claude/rules/household-config.md`), so back on, nothing from while off fires late — a low still going included (deliberate); features that cannot be read count as **on** — a medical alarm must not go silent because the accounts service blinked. The poller writes and the triggers read the link's `LastSuccessAt` and `FailingSince` (kept from the first failure, or a `for` never elapses): `no_readings` is judged on LibreLinkUp's last answer, never while the link fails, `link_failing` on `FailingSince`. A trigger fires on entering its condition (after `for`) and not again until it left; one with no state row is judged without firing, so a watch written while she is low fires at the next crossing — the `glucose-watches` skill teaches the same rule; one that cannot be judged now keeps its row, or gets none, since a recorded "not holding" would fake a crossing (`GlucoseTriggers`). Time conditions read the zone her phones last saved (`HomeCalendar.OnSaved`) and gate the fire, not the episode (a low begun before the window fires when it opens).
- **A fire** runs `IGlucoseWatchEffects`: a `prompt` composed by `FirePlanning` (`GlucoseWatchFire`, `GlucoseFireReport`, sender `ServiceSenders.GlucoseWatch`) emitted gate-on-live on Sugary's channel, and an `announce` via `IGlucoseAnnounceEffect` (`GlucoseAnnounce`: satellites as an `Alarm`, else an insistent channel alert). **A fire that reached nobody leaves its episode unfired** (reached = a prompt an agent took or an announcement rung): the next judgement still finding the condition tries again (one attempt a judgement), one finding it left says nothing late. `LastFiredAt` and a `once` watch's `Spent` come only from a fire that reached somebody, written in one transaction guarded on the watch's `Spec`, `Enabled`, `!Spent` and state-row count being what was read.
- **A watch's prompt runs as the person whose turn last wrote it**, on the agent's delegation (`.claude/rules/delegations.md`, "Deferred turns"): the agent signs one for a write to `sugary-watches` (`DelegatedWrites`, described by `GlucoseWatchDelegation` as a `watch` — the home watch's description, since the agent checks the same of both); the mount keeps it only when it names this watch and writer (`GlucoseWatchDelegation.KeptFor`), each write replacing it, and a write carrying none leaves the watch nobody's. `userId` is a label the agent does not believe (it believes the delegation, `GlucoseWatchFire`); it must be the writer, the subject, or what the watch already said, else refused naming the field. A fire's zone stays the phones' saved one: the accounts service accepts a delegation as proof of a household only for a schedule, from the scheduling server.
- **What the agent is told** is Sugary's (`AddSkills`): `sugary-log` names the `glucose` and `sensors` tables and sends no glucose question to `/ha`; `glucose-watches` (`Domain/Prompts/Skills/glucose-watches.md`) teaches the watch file. Its description and the home's `home-watches` description and guide stub divide by subject (a person's sensor vs the home's entities); a misspelled kind falls to the home (the union's fallback).
- **`import-glucose`** (`GlucoseImport`, `GlucoseImportCommand` shows the invocation): unlike `import`, its rows are the named person's, never `legacy`; a rerun adds nothing. The token is read from `--token-file` and never printed; `--token` is refused. Imported rows never reach `IGlucosePollObserver`.
- **The Glucose tab** (`GlucoseEndpoints`) reads by GETs, never queued behind her writes. Readings are kept in mg/dL everywhere; mmol/L is mg/dL ÷ 18.016 to one decimal in both the app (`glucose.ts`) and the mount. `/statistics` is `GlucoseStatistics` and the README's glucose statements are `GlucoseQueries` — **both time-weighted the same way** (each reading weighs the minutes to the next, at most `GlucoseQueries.MaxMinutesPerReading`; the app's chart breaks its line at the same step), so a change to one is made to both (`GlucoseStatisticsParityTests`). Wire shape the app relies on: `/current` answers a null link without one, `canLink`, and mg/dL against 70–180 without a link; `/readings` answers every reading up to two days, quarter-hour means up to sixteen days, hourly beyond (`GlucoseCurve`), and omits an empty bucket so the chart breaks its line (`bad-window`, `window-too-long` past 90 days); `/statistics?days=` takes 1, 7, 14 or 90 (else `bad-period`, the app's `PERIODS`) and is null but count and coverage with no readings; its shares are of covered time and its bands cumulative (under 70 includes under 54, over 180 includes over 250; `types.ts`), in range being her range. A refusal's `code` reaches the app on `ApiError.code`.

## Who she is

`Profile` (`/api/profile`) and `BodyMeasurement` (`/api/measurements`): each profile field null until she says it, a field sent empty taken back, a diagnosis before birth refused; height (cm) and weight (kg) one of each kind a day (a second refused), the kind never changes. Both are queued log writes. The app's **Cycle tab is hidden only from a man who said so**; somebody who never said keeps it, and her periods stay logged and mounted either way. No paging: `GET /measurements` returns every entry.

**A day of her calendar** (`/api/day-notes/{day}`, `DayNote`) is her words, a flow and symptoms, codes from `DayEntries` (the app's `days.ts`, same order; the mount's `day_notes` `flow`/`symptoms` columns), kept while any is said, deleted when nothing is. The app always sends `symptoms` and `loaded` (merged per field, as above); **a save without `symptoms` is an app from before them** and changes the words alone.

## Turns, the mount and reminders

- **Sugary turned off refuses the app.** The agent refuses a Sugary turn of a Sugary-off household (`RefusalAnswers.SugaryOff`), and the server answers the app's estimate or label **403** with the refusal's code (`RefusalAnswers.CodeOf`) whenever the agent refused for the household; the app words each code in her language (`errors.ts`), never the agent's English. Any other failure stays 502.
- An estimate's and a label's turn carry `Principal("sugary", <user id>, Account)` with `Sender` the account's id (no resolution call per meal), and the request's assertion (`Person.Assertion`) as the notification's `Assertion`, which the agent checks for channel `sugary` too. `OpenEstimates` lets only the person who opened a meal's conversation continue it; anyone else is the same 410 as a closed one.
- **Sugary is on for a person once an owner says so** (user's decision, 2026-11-19): `User.UsesSugary`, the members page's "Uses Sugary", off for somebody new, on for everyone there when it arrived (the `SugaryUsers` migration's column default — the store writes it on every person it makes, so the default reaches only old rows); `create-user --sugary` makes one on (the test stacks seed theirs so). Sugary reads it from the `sugary-people` view (`SugaryPerson.UsesSugary`, read **on** when an answer leaves it out). Off, the app is refused (`SugaryAccess`, below), the mount refuses their own log `permission_denied`, and nobody sees them under `people/` or may name them in a watch. A person the view does not list is let through to their own log (only a stand-in with no people omits one: a disabled member's session never gets here). The work that wakes for no request reads it from the `sugary-off` view's `people` (`SugaryOff`): their link is not polled (seen again as a new link once on), their watches judge nothing and forget what was judged (as a Sugary-off household's), their pens remind nobody; unreadable, everyone is on.
- **Everyone's log, for a reader an owner granted it** (user's decision, 2026-11-12: one member's agent running analytics on another's diabetes): `User.ReadsSugaryLogs`, owner-granted like sees-all, never a role; each account page names who holds it. The mount asks `ICallHousehold.SugaryPeopleAsync` and, when the reader is granted and the household is the reader's own, shows each other person Sugary is on for under `people/<folder>/` (`SugaryPath.FolderOf`); a query at or under `people/<folder>` reads that person's whole log, anywhere else the reader's own. The owner's `Account` is built from the reader's tenant and the view's user id, **never parsed from a path**. Without the grant there is no `people/` in a listing and anything under it is `permission_denied`, never empty. The view is kept a minute (no Redis), so a withdrawn grant still reads for up to a minute.
- **The app shows a reader everyone's log, read only** (user's decisions, 2026-11-19): `SugaryAccess`, in the `/api` gate after the assertion, asks the people view on the request's assertion (`CallHousehold.OfPerson`) and decides once per request: Sugary off and no grant is **403 `sugary-not-yours`** on every call; a reader names a person with `?person=<user id>` (every call, the photo, export and CSV URLs included) and `Person.Log` becomes that person's account — the reader's tenant plus the view's id, only somebody Sugary is on for — on a GET or HEAD alone (any other method **403 `viewing-read-only`**, a person not offered **403 `not-granted`**, never an empty log); a reader Sugary is off for has no log, so only `/session` and the pantry answer them without `person`. **The pantry is the household's** (`/foods`, `/recipes`, `/products`, `/labels`): anyone the app admits reads and writes it, and `person` says nothing there. A view that cannot be read is **503 `people-unreadable`**. `SugaryDbContext.BindAsync` and the exports read `Person.Log`; anything that writes, estimates, labels, links or subscribes a phone stays `Person.Account`. `/api/session` answers `usesSugary` and `people` (whom the reader may pick, empty without the grant); the app's picker, the viewing mode and the "Sugary is not on for you" screen are the client's (below).
- The `/sugary` mount answers for the account on the call's `_meta` principal (`SugaryFileSystem.For`, `Principal.Of(caller.Conversation)?.Account`), never an ambient. A call naming nobody is refused `permission_denied`, **never shown an empty log** (a false fact about a health record); `ReadBlobAsync` is overridden to return that refusal rather than throw it from the stream. The eval stamps every turn with `EvalRun.Account`.
- Mount tables `profile`, `heights`, `weights`, `pens` (an unsaid field an empty cell, never a default); the README and the `sugary-log` skill say **none is a number to dose from**: a weight or an insulin's name invites a ratio or an action time she never gave.
- `glucose/<YYYY-MM>.parquet` and `sensors.parquet` (`ends_at` = activation + `SugaryTables.SensorLifetime`); the month files come from `ISugaryLog.GlucoseMonthsAsync`, each month's first reading by index, never every instant. `query` leaves `glucose` out only of a statement naming other tables and never glucose (`SugaryFileSystem.OtherTable`); every catalog listing gets it, and left out it is absent, never empty. Every read is one version (`IReadSnapshots`, `.claude/rules/virtual-filesystem.md`, "A mount with tables answers SQL"). The README is per log (`SugaryReadme.For(zone, targets)`).
- A push subscription's key is its endpoint (one browser): subscribing as somebody else reassigns it, so her reminders stop reaching that phone (`PushEndpoints`). A phone hears only while the session that subscribed it lives (`PushSubscription.SessionId`): `PenReminders` asks the accounts service (`ISessionAccounts`, `/accounts/internal/session` on Sugary's own channel) before telling it anything, drops a phone whose session ended, and **tells nothing to one it could not ask about just now**. Reminders go to the owner's phones only, on `PushSubscription.TimeZone`.
- **Her clock is her household's** (`settings.timeZone`, `.claude/rules/household-config.md`): the mount reads times in the call's household zone (`FileSystemCaller.Household`), a route's `HomeCalendar` is bound per request (`HomeCalendar.BindAsync`, proved by `CallHousehold.OfPerson`). `HomeZone` (compose `TZ`) is the fallback for an unknown zone or a phone that never saved one. The mount's description names no zone (it is served before any call).

## The client

- A 401 sends the page to `/accounts/sign-in?returnUrl=<here>` (`signin.ts`). A 403 carrying `X-Ziggurat-Refusal: suspended` (`SessionCheck.RefusalHeader`, copied back by forward_auth, never on the app's own 403s — `isSuspension`) sends it to `/accounts/account?returnUrl=<here>`.
- The service worker passes `/accounts/*` navigations to the network (`navigateFallbackDenylist`) or sign-in loops; the manifest is fetched `useCredentials` since the session check guards it too.
- **The unsaved meal is hers alone** (`useDraft(person)`, `draftFor`): the draft names `person` from `/api/session`; a phone signed in as somebody else finds none and removes hers; a draft naming no person, or an app opened offline, shows none. Ration size, language, theme and entry mode are the device's; profile, measurements and pens are hers, on the server.
- **A desktop is one media query** (`min-width: 64rem`, end of `styles.css`): the tabs become a rail, a screen's `.panes`/`.pane`/`.cards` wrappers lay it out side by side, sheets open centred. Below it those wrappers are `display: contents`, so the phone's layout and DOM order stay as they were; a wrapper only ever groups sections already adjacent in DOM order. The tabs stay one `<nav aria-label="Sugary">` (the E2E tests find them by it). A chart draws in `chartBox` (`chart.ts`): scaled on a phone, drawn wider rather than larger past one.
- **Whose log** (`viewing.ts`, `setViewing`/`withPerson` in `api.ts`): the picker is drawn only when `/api/session`'s `people` offers somebody besides the signer, so anyone without the grant gets no DOM or layout change (the phone screenshots stay byte-identical). Picking somebody else adds `person=<id>` to every address the app asks (photo, export and CSV URLs too), remounts the shell for that id and re-reads their profile; it shows every tab but Meal (Cycle as their profile says): Log, Glucose, Cycle and Settings read only (every add, edit, delete, link and estimate affordance hidden, profile fields disabled, no reminders; ration size, language and theme stay the device's), and the Pantry read-write with no add-to-meal, since it is the household's; it never resyncs push and keeps the meal draft the signer's. Pantry addresses never carry `person` (`HOUSEHOLD` in `api.ts`). A reader with no log of their own opens on the first person offered. A 403 `sugary-not-yours` on `/session` (or no log and nobody offered) is the "Sugary isn't on for you" screen, never a sign-in loop.
- `npm run dev` proxies `/api` as the proxy would: with `SUGARY_SESSION` set to a `mint-session` cookie it asks `ACCOUNTS_URL` (default `http://localhost:5004`) for `channel=sugary` before each call and copies the four headers it answers onto the request; unset, every call is 401.
